Our Story

15+ Years of Dedicated Engineering in Kollam

Founded with a vision to deliver world-class technology solutions to South Kerala, Teknix - The Technology Destination has grown into one of Kollam's most respected Authorised IT & POS Service Centers.

Located conveniently at 49/380, Palakassery Building, High School Junction, we house an ESD-safe micro-soldering laboratory, multi-brand printer diagnostic benches, and an enterprise networking deployment crew.

Our Vision
