About Teknix

History of Our Company

Founded in 2004, TEKNIX has established itself as a premier provider of IT infrastructure services in Kollam. With over 19 years of experience, we specialize in supporting medium to small corporate, government, and semi-government organizations with top-notch IT solutions. Our expertise spans computer systems, printers, laptops, and peripherals, ensuring comprehensive support for diverse technology environments.

Our Mission:

“To deliver high-quality services at competitive prices, fostering successful relationships with our customers and suppliers.”

Our Approach:

“At TEKNIX, We believe in continuous improvement and integration. Our strong quality management system enables us to provide exceptional solutions.”
