# Teknix - The Technology Destination (Official Website)

> **Authorised IT & POS Service Center | Kollam, Kerala**  
> Redesigned as a multi-page website using Teknix's official logo purple theme and smooth scroll entrance animations.

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture

1. **Multi-Page Navigation (No Single-Page Scroll)**:
   - [index.html](file:///d:/tools/technix/index.html) – Home page with Hero, Brand Partners, Service Highlights, Instant Estimator & Why Teknix.
   - [about.html](file:///d:/tools/technix/about.html) – About Us, 15+ years engineering heritage in Kollam, mission, and core values.
   - [services.html](file:///d:/tools/technix/services.html) – Comprehensive services breakdown with category filtering (Laptops, Printers, Cabling, POS, AMC).
   - [amc.html](file:///d:/tools/technix/amc.html) – Corporate & Institutional AMC packages, SLAs, and preventive maintenance terms.
   - [gallery.html](file:///d:/tools/technix/gallery.html) – Workstation photos, diagnostic lab benches, and retail POS installations.
   - [contact.html](file:///d:/tools/technix/contact.html) – Contact details (High School Junction, Kollam), direct hotlines, message form, and interactive Google Map.

2. **Official Logo Purple Theme**:
   - Clean, modern light palette with crisp white surfaces and soft lavender backgrounds.
   - Official Teknix brand royal purple (`#68228b`, `#832fb0`, `#4d1566`) matching the logo branding.
   - Transparent high-resolution official logo integration.

3. **Smooth Scroll & Load Animations**:
   - `IntersectionObserver`-powered scroll reveal effects (`.reveal`, `.reveal-left`, `.reveal-right`, staggered delays).
   - Smooth entrance animations as sections and cards scroll into view.
   - Interactive hover elevation and button ripples.

4. **Interactive Utilities**:
   - Live Repair Cost & Turnaround Estimator with auto-generated WhatsApp quote messaging.
   - Category filter for services.
   - Direct-dial phone links (`0474 2748888`, `+91 89432 94838`, `+91 94473 42015`).
   - Floating WhatsApp contact button.
   - Global booking modal with form validation and confirmation toast.
